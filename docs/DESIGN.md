## Brand & Style

This design system embodies mindful luxury, sensory warmth, and contemporary editorial elegance. Designed for an artisanal aromatic candle house, the experience balances serene, breathable space with tangible sensory romance. It caters to a conscious, aesthetically discerning audience that values slow living, ambient interior rituals, and craftsmanship.

The visual language marries **Sensory Warm Minimalism** with **Editorial Softness**. Interfaces prioritize pristine clarity, generous breathing room, and soft tactile transitions over visual density. Micro-interactions should feel whisper-quiet, fluid, and intentional—emulating the flicker of candlelight and the diffuse unfurling of botanical scents.

## Colors

The palette establishes an airy sanctuary dominated by clean alabaster, delicate ivory, and warm cream, accented by soothing lavender mist and punctive botanical fuchsia.

- **Canvas & Whites:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) serves as the base for interactive cards and clean cutouts, grounded upon warm ivory (`#FAF9F6`) and delicate cream (`#FDFBF7`) for overarching canvas backgrounds and sectional shifts.
- **Lavenders (Primary & Atmospheric Accents):** Soft mist lavender (`#E6E1FA`) handles ethereal tags, hover tints, and delicate fills. Wisteria violet (`#9D8DF1`) and deep dusk purple (`#7E6CCA`) anchor structural primaries, active brand touchpoints, links, and key CTA borders.
- **Pastel Fuchsia (Vibrant Micro-Highlights):** Vibrant peony (`#F472B6`) and radiant fuchsia (`#EC4899`) are deployed sparingly as high-resonance focal points: sale badges, price accents, selected states, and olfactory intensity notes.
- **Text & Contrast:** Charcoal slate (`#2D283E`) ensures legibility with high softness, avoiding jarring optical weight, paired with muted lilac-gray (`#787289`) for subtext and technical candle specifications.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy creates an immediate sense of artisanal refinement by pairing a classic, high-contrast transitional serif with a geometric, humanist sans-serif.

- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys bespoke craftsmanship, warmth, and timeless editorial character. Headings adopt gentle tracking and organic letterforms, honoring the poetry of scent notes and collection titles.
- **Body & Product Information (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Guarantees pristine legibility across functional specifications (wax composition, burn duration, net weight, notes pyramid). Its open counters prevent visual fatigue.
- **Labels & Metatags:** Rendered in uppercase or elevated weight using Plus Jakarta Sans with generous letter-spacing to impart an understated luxury perfume house feel.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model relies on a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid designed around rhythmic, open proportions.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a minimum 48px (`3rem`) canvas margin. Product matrices span 3 or 4 columns per card to maintain generous pictorial breathing room.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns with 20px gutters and 32px canvas margins. Product grids collapse gracefully to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) lateral padding. Single-card or asymmetric carousel layouts replace tight grids to prevent visual clutter.
- **Rhythm & Proportions:** Vertical flow uses progressive scalar spacing (`space-md` within grouped elements, `space-xl` between modular sections) to cultivate a calm, unhurried cadence reminiscent of high-end art books.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects heavy, aggressive drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering**, **Low-Contrast Micro-Borders**, and **Atmospheric Ambient Glows**.

- **Surfaces & Borders:** Baseline depth is achieved by resting `#FFFFFF` components on `#FAF9F6` backgrounds, demarcated by ultra-fine hairline outlines (`rgba(126, 108, 202, 0.12)` or `rgba(45, 40, 62, 0.06)`).
- **Ambient Glows:** Elevated elements (such as modal sheets, floating carts, and hovering product cards) utilize ultra-diffused, tinted atmospheric shadows: `0 16px 36px -12px rgba(126, 108, 202, 0.08)`.
- **Translucency:** Sticky navigation bars and floating filters use frosted glass surfaces with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over an 85% opacity `#FAF9F6` base to evoke ethereal, mist-like transparency.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, sculpted curvature (`roundedness: 2`, where base radius is 8px / `0.5rem`, `rounded-lg` is 16px / `1rem`, and `rounded-xl` is 24px / `1.5rem`).

This curvature mirrors the organic silhouettes of hand-poured candle vessels, smooth wax pools, and ceramic holders. Sharp edges are reserved only for subtle visual divider rules, while all interactive surfaces, inputs, pills, and imagery adopt gentle, welcoming contours.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled dusk purple (`#7E6CCA`) with pure white text (`#FFFFFF`), subtle `0.5rem` radius, and horizontal padding of `1.5rem`. On hover, transitions smoothly to `#6D5BB6` accompanied by an ultra-soft lavender ambient glow.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` background framed by a 1px border of `#9D8DF1`, using dusk purple typography. Hover transitions to `#FAF9F6` with a border tint of `#7E6CCA`.
- **Accent / Micro-CTA:** Clean link button in vibrant fuchsia (`#EC4899`) with a sliding underline effect for curated recommendations.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Construction:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card faces bordered by a 1px clean stroke (`rgba(126, 108, 202, 0.15)`), rounded to `1rem`.
- **Imagery:** Aspect ratios of 4:5 or 1:1 with soft rounded tops (`1rem`). Hover states trigger a subtle image scale (1.02x) over 400ms ease.
- **Price Presentation:** Current retail prices are rendered in vibrant fuchsia (`#EC4899`) in medium weight (`Plus Jakarta Sans`), standing distinct against charcoal slate product titles.

### Chips & Olfactory Badges
- **Scent Note Tags:** Rendered in pastel lavender fill (`#E6E1FA`) with deep purple text (`#7E6CCA`), pill-shaped (`2rem` radius), using `label-md` typography.
- **Status Pills (Limited Batch / Hand-Poured):** Translucent fuchsia wash (`rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.08)`) with vibrant fuchsia borders and text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Fields:** Subtle cream fill (`#FDFBF7`) enclosed by a 1px hairline border (`rgba(45, 40, 62, 0.12)`), radius `0.5rem`. Focused states illuminate with a 1px `#9D8DF1` boundary and a 3px lavender focus ring (`rgba(230, 225, 250, 0.5)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved containers with dusk purple active fills and clean white checkmarks. Unchecked states carry an ivory background with soft lavender borders.

### Sensory Accordions & Fragrance Pyramids
- Tailored component for Top, Heart, and Base fragrance notes. Minimalist divider lines (`rgba(126, 108, 202, 0.1)`) separate tiers, expanding with graceful vertical ease to show botanical ingredient sourcing and burn profiles.
